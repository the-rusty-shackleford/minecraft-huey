## The dimensions

The numbers the model is built to (metres; facts, not anyone's drawing):

- fuselage, nose to tail: 12.62; main rotor 14.71 across, two blades of 0.533 chord; tail rotor
  2.59 across, its hub 3.11 up, on the left of the fin;
- the mast 3.57 behind the nose; the rotor head's top 4.21 up; the stabilizer bar 2.76 across;
- skid tread 2.61; the cabin floor 0.61 up; the cargo opening 1.88 long and 1.22 high; the cabin
  floor 2.44 wide inside; the elevator 2.85 across, 0.78 chord, 1.45 up.

From the dimensions table and Figure 2.1 of Eagle Dynamics' *DCS: UH-1H Huey Flight Manual*, which
reproduces the U.S. Army operator's manual's principal dimensions. That figure's drawing does not
match its own labels everywhere (it puts the mast 4.0 behind the nose), so where they disagree the
labels and Goebel's profile, which agree, were taken. Heights the labels don't give (the cabin roof
2.3, the engine cowling 2.6, the boom 1.8 to 1.9 at its top) are read off Goebel's profile at a
scale fitted to the labelled heights.
