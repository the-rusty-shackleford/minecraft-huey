# D-0002 — The baggage store: a double chest in the boom, a hatch each side

Rusty, after flying it (2026-10-07): the helicopters should have storage like the cars, and "a
double chest would be nice. I am okay with not actually putting a whole double chest in the huey if
it still offers the same storage space as a double chest"; then "opening inventory while in the
craft should show its inventory as well, like the cars". The Chinook had two chests from the start;
the Huey had none, so a rider's inventory key opened nothing at all.

**Options he was shown.** The cabin has no floor to spare: the rear bench leans on the bulkhead, the
front bench and the pilots' seats take the rest, and a chest behind the pilots would sit where the
front row's feet go. So (1) the store hidden behind the cabin, opened at a hatch, or (2) a
two-thirds chest on the cabin floor, legs clipping into it. He chose 1.

**Decision**:

- **Where.** The cargo doors are drawn slid back over the aft fuselage (4.30 to 6.18), so the skin
  just behind the cabin is the doors, and from there the fuselage steps down fast into the boom. The
  first flat skin is the boom's forward end: the hatch is there, 0.70 long and 0.60 high (stations
  6.88 to 7.58), on both sides, a dark frame a pixel or less proud of the skin with a latch at its
  aft edge, and the store is the game's double chest at 0.45 inside the boom, six rows, its long side
  along it. Its lid, opened, stays inside the boom. Whether the real UH-1H has a baggage door here
  was not confirmed; the place is chosen for the model, not claimed for the aircraft.
- **The click.** A third hit box covers the hatches (1.0 square, 1.0 high, at 7.23), so a click on
  either one lands on the vehicle and Vanilla Wheels follows its line to the store, as a chest in a
  bed is reached through the hull over it. Without that box nothing covered the hatches.
- **The inventory key** needs nothing of its own: Vanilla Wheels opens a vehicle's first chest for a
  rider who presses it, as in the cars, and the store is the first chest.

**Verified**: GameTest `itsBaggageStoreOpensFromEitherHatchAndFromTheSeat` (each hatch through the
client's interaction packet, six rows; a click on the cabin still boards; aboard, the inventory key
opens it), which failed with the hatch's hit box taken out. The booth films the hatch, the store
opened from it, and the store opened by a real E key from the pilot's seat.
