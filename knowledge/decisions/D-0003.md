# D-0003 — Heard from afar, seen through clear

Rusty's friends, 2026-10-22: no helicopter could be heard flying in third person, and one asked to
hear them going by from two hundred blocks; in first person the glass was "causing some sort of
visual bug" under shaders.

**Decision**:
- The rotor's `attenuation_distance` is 200 (it was 64): it fades evenly from the aircraft out to
  nothing at 200 blocks, half as loud at a hundred (Rusty's pick over 128 and a quieter idle). The
  third-person silence was not the range: every vehicle had played the car's petrol engine at sixteen
  blocks (Vanilla Wheels' D-0045).
- The glass is the cockpit's too (`"cockpit": {"group": ["cockpit", "glass"]}`, Vanilla Wheels'
  D-0031): drawn for everyone else, not for a rider looking out through their own eyes. Faded in front
  of the eye as before, Iris drew the pane and not the world behind it: under Complementary, no
  clouds or haze through the windows and a flat, banded sky.

How far one is heard also needs the listener's client to have it: the server sends an aircraft to
anyone within its view distance (24 chunks on the box), but no further than the chunks their client
loads.

**Verification**: the booth checks the loop playing on the client is `huey:rotor` at 200 blocks. The
glass was photographed in first person on the 4070 under Complementary with the old selector and the
new (not kept: the booth runs in software).
