# D-0001 — The Huey: life-size, by script, origin under the mast

Rusty asked (2026-10-06) for a Huey and a Chinook, dyeable, flying with their own up and down,
carrying a sling load, and chose life-size (one block a metre). Astra is gone; the model is ours to
make.

**Decision**:

- **Scale and shape.** A Bell UH-1H at one block a metre, built in metres from the published
  dimensions (fuselage 12.62, main rotor 14.71 with 0.533 blades, tail rotor 2.59 on the fin's left
  with its hub 3.11 up, mast 3.57 behind the nose, cabin floor 0.61 up, cargo opening 1.88 long,
  skid tread 2.61, elevator 2.85 across at 1.45) and checked against Greg Goebel's public-domain
  profile, which agrees with them; heights the tables don't give are read off his profile at a
  scale fitted to those they do (`devtools/art/reference/SOURCES.md`). An overlay of the profile on
  a side render at the same frame lands within a few pixels along the whole length.
- **The look.** The cockpit doors shut with their windows; the cargo doors slid back over the aft
  fuselage, as in the 1971 photograph, so the riders show, sitting on red webbing troop seats; dark
  chin windows; the green-tinted roof windows; the cowling with its grille, inspection windows,
  beacon and round exhaust; a two-bladed rotor with its stabilizer bar; skids on arched cross tubes.
  Olive drab from the factory (`#4f5536`), the paint group dyed whole; skids, blades and metal are
  not paint.
- **Made by script** (`devtools/art/build.py` over the shared `tools/bbgen`): cubes in named
  folders, one texel a model pixel, so every face has the same grain (bbgen's `TexelAtlas`). The
  nose is lofted in layers a pixel thick, a bulb 1.72 across flaring to the cabin's 2.4 behind it;
  a fin and a boom taper in pixel steps.
- **Its origin is on the ground under the mast**: it lands on its skids and turns about its rotor,
  and Vanilla Wheels' body box stands under the cabin. The tail is nine metres behind it, so the
  Rotorcraft profile names a `hull` of six boxes and the rotors' radii (Rotorcraft's D-0001).
- **Seats**: the pilot in the right-hand seat (the UH-1's pilot in command), a copilot, and two
  rows of three facing forward in the cabin, all at a cushion 0.94 up so a seated head clears the
  roof.
- **The sound**: a loop of thirty blade beats (94 ms each, a UH-1's two blades at 324 rpm) cut
  from a CC0 recording, its note 0.62 to 1.04 (Vanilla Wheels' `sounds.pitch`, added for it: a
  car's note would play the beat at 60 ms), heard 64 blocks off.

**Why**: life-size is Rusty's call; published dimensions make the proportions facts rather than
guesses; scripting the model keeps it reviewable and regenerable, and lets every change be checked
by rendering before anyone flies it.

**Alternatives rejected**: *a Blockbench file by hand* (nobody to make it, and no way to diff it);
*the origin at the hull's middle* (it would land on its tail boom's root and yaw about a point
behind the cabin); *the cargo doors shut* (riders hidden; the open Huey is the one people know).

**Verification**: `HueyGameTests` (5): the profiles, the chassis recipe, the lift's build and paint,
a flown circuit landing at full condition with the rider unhurt, and the Sling Container carried and
set down whole. Not yet seen: the model in the game (the booth), the sound in the game, Rusty's eye.
